One image that sums up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026, drawn as
the front page of a one-page newspaper, "The World Today". The lead story is
the first trilateral meeting of the United States, Chinese and Russian
presidents, held in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Around it:
the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya, Ukraine's third strike of the week on the Crimean
Bridge, Israeli tanks pushing into central Gaza, and the COP31 talks
deadlocked over the fossil-fuel phase-out, with a ticker of the day's other
stories along the foot of the page.

The image is composed by `compose.py` (a build-time script, Pillow) and the
submitted program, `main.py`, copies the finished `entry.png` to the output
path using only the Python standard library, so it runs offline and writes
exactly the same bytes every time.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
